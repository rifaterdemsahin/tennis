Basics 3 of 6

Backhand — step by step

Start with two hands. The non-dominant hand does the work.

Step 1 — Both hands, turn

Keep both hands on the handle. Turn so your back shoulder points at the net. This is not a one-hand slap.

Step 2 — Contact in front

Hit the ball in front of your body. The left hand (if you are right-handed) pushes through. Do not scoop under the ball or you will dump it in the net.

Step 3 — Finish and recover

Finish toward the opposite shoulder. Then split-step back to ready. Cross-court backhands (one diagonal) are the drill we use on Saturdays.
