Basics 5 of 6

Volley — step by step

At the net the swing gets smaller, not bigger.

Step 1 — Close the net

Move in after a good approach. Racket head up, in front of your chest. This is a punch, not a groundstroke.

Step 2 — Block / punch

Meet the ball in front. Short step, firm wrist, tiny backswing. A big loop at the net sends the ball long or into the fence.

Step 3 — Cover your half

In doubles, one at net, one back is classic. Talk: “yours / mine”. Poach only if you can actually reach it.
