Venues

Courts we actually use

Public and pay-and-play courts around Cambridge and St Ives. Parking is arranged privately — never post gate codes or home addresses on this site.

Court Why it matters How to play
Jesus Green
Cambridge city centre, by the Cam		 Default city option. Four hard courts. Parktennis sometimes Saturday mornings. Not always free — group has been bumped when it was not booked. Reserve and pay via Cambridge City Council / ClubSpark. Jesus Green on ClubSpark. Map: ///pens.parks.bubble
Christ’s Pieces
off King Street		 Handy if you are already in town. Same booking family as Jesus Green. Walking routes across the park are short (~0.8 km) if you stay on the diagonal, not the long loop. Reserve via ClubSpark / city courts. Location: ///budget.silver.heavy. Council overview: cambridge.gov.uk tennis courts
St Ives outdoor courts
One Leisure Outdoor, California Road		 Group favourite so far: good shade, “heaven” on a hot day. Court scout unlocked this venue. After rain, surface can be messy. Allow travel time from Cambridge — a scenic detour can double the walk. 2 outdoor courts, also used for netball. One Leisure St Ives Outdoor. What3Words: ///albatross.delivers.ages
Lammas Land
Fen Causeway		 City fallback with 2 public outdoor courts when the centre courts are full. Check Cambridge City Council court list before you travel.

On the day

  • Arrive with a racket. Balls are pooled; do not assume someone else packed the last can.
  • Park-and-walk spots exist a few minutes from some courts. Ask in the private group — they are not listed here.
  • Tea after play is encouraged. Plastic cups solve the picnic problem.
