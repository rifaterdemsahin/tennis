🎒 First Saturday checklist

1. Kit

  • 🎾 Any racket (borrow one in the chat if you have none)
  • 👟 Trainers with a decent sole — not smooth-soled office shoes
  • 💧 Water. Optional: cap, sunscreen

2. Balls

Practice / pressureless balls are fine for learning. Match cans die fast. If you have a fresh can, bring it. Do not assume the last set is still in someone’s bag.

3. Booking

We use the Play Tennis app. £3 per 45-minute slot at Christ's Pieces. Erdem pays Saturdays. Weekdays: whoever books, pays or splits.

4. Gate

PIN is in the app after checkout — not on this website. If you arrive early, wait; do not publish the code.

🔐 Tennis day: unlock the gate

Cambridge City Council courts (Christ's Pieces, Jesus Green) use ClubSpark / LTA SmartAccess — an electronic keypad. Your code is only in the Play Tennis app after you book.

Enter the star first

Type * then your PIN from the app, with no space. Example shape only: *XXXXXX — never post the live code on this site or in a public chat.

Timing / grace period

The PIN turns on 10–15 minutes before your start (e.g. 09:15) and stays live until shortly after your end (e.g. 10:00). It will not work outside that window — don’t arrive half an hour early expecting the gate to open.

Automatic latching

The gate locks when it closes. You do not need a code to exit from the inside. Don’t hold the gate open for the next booking unless they have their own PIN.

🖐️ The five shots that matter

Shot What to aim for Starter mistake
Forehand Side-on, racket low to high, finish over the opposite shoulder. Hit in front of your body. Facing the net square-on and slapping late.
Backhand Two hands is easier at the start (left hand drives). Turn the shoulders; do not scoop. Bending the wrist and lifting the ball short.
Serve Start with a simple overhead from the baseline into the opposite service box. Toss in front, not behind your head. A full jump serve on day one. Consistency first.
Volley At the net: short punch, racket head up, no big swing. Taking a full groundstroke swing at the net.
Return Split step as they toss. Block it back deep rather than going for a winner. Standing on the baseline frozen, swinging from the heels.

🔢 Scoring in one minute

We often rally without score. When someone says “let’s keep score”:

  • Points go 0 → 15 → 30 → 40 → game.
  • If it is 40–40, that is deuce. Win two points in a row: advantage, then game. (Or play “next point wins” if the slot is ending.)
  • Six games wins a set, by two. We rarely finish a full set in one 45-minute booking — that is fine.
  • Server starts from the right (deuce) court, then left (ad) court, alternating.
  • Call the score before each point, server first: “30–15”. Honest line calls: if you are not sure, it was in.

🤝 Doubles (our default)

Four people = a match. Two at the net, two at the back is the classic shape, but learners can start both back.

  • Talk: “yours”, “mine”, “switch”. Silence loses points.
  • Poach only if you can actually reach it. Otherwise stay in your half.
  • Serve from the right to start a game; partners swap sides each game.
  • If a fifth person arrives, rotate every two games so nobody sits for long.

🔁 A 45-minute starter session

0–10 min · Warm rally

Co-operative. Aim for 5–7 balls in a row over the net. No winners. Feed from the hand if the serve is not there yet.

10–25 min · Cross-court

Forehands only, then backhands only. Stay on one diagonal. This is how a backhand gets “solid” without a lesson.

25–40 min · Mini games

Play to 7 points. Serve underarm if you need to. Keep the score out loud — that is the skill we asked people back for in September.

Last 5 · Pack up

Collect every ball. If the next booking is waiting, leave the court. Tea / picnic if people have time.

🧒 Juniors

When kids are in town we try to book a separate court so they can play and enjoy it — not squeeze into adult doubles unplanned. Mini tennis (red / orange balls) is better than a full yellow ball if they are small.

🚫 Court manners

  • Priority to whoever booked. If you drop in unpaid, offer to share or leave when they arrive.
  • Quiet early mornings. Take litter. Do not walk behind a court mid-point.
  • Racket is not ping-pong: it is heavier. Warm the arm; do not smash 50 serves cold.

This is a learners’ cheat sheet, not a coaching qualification. If you want proper lessons later, Cambridge has academies listed on the city council tennis page. With us: show up, say you are new, we rally.
