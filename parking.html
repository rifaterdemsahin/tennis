Free parking at Petersfield Mansions 🅿️

Erdem has free spaces at Petersfield Mansions, Cambridge CB1 1BB. Ring No 50 at the basin / gate and he will open. Then walk about 15 minutes to Christ's Pieces tennis courts.

Ask before you assume a bay is free. A few cars fit. Do not block neighbours. Ring 50 — do not publish other door codes.

How to park

Navigate to Petersfield Mansions

Postcode CB1 1BB. The building sits on Petersfield, just off East Road, east of the city centre (near the Grafton / Parkside side of town).

Ring No 50

At the basin / entry, press 50. Erdem opens the parking. Say you are here for tennis.

Leave time to walk

PIN on the tennis gate only works 10–15 minutes before the booking. Park, then walk — don’t sit in the car until 09:15.

Walk to Christ's Pieces (~15 min)

Take the short diagonal. The long loop via Grafton / Willow Walk / New Square roughly doubles the walk (the group already learned that the hard way).

  1. Leave Petersfield Mansions and walk out toward East Road.
  2. Turn toward the city and join Parkside (the wide road along the park). Keep left of the long detour up to the Grafton.
  3. Enter Christ's Pieces and cut diagonally across the grass paths toward the northwest corner.
  4. The four hard courts sit on the King Street side of the park (what3words ///budget.silver.heavy). Unlock with * + the PIN in Play Tennis.
  5. After play, walk the same diagonal back. No code needed to exit the tennis gate.
