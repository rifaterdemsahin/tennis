📧 Cambridge City Council Email Receipt

What email you get after registering for the court

When you book a court at Christ's Pieces or Jesus Green via Play Tennis / ClubSpark, you immediately receive an official payment confirmation and receipt from Cambridge City Council.

Always complete these two key actions before arriving at the courts:

📸 Sample Email Receipt

Here is the exact email receipt generated after registering and paying for a court slot at Christ's Pieces:

🧾 Receipt Details Breakdown

  • Sender: Cambridge <receipts+acct_...> (via Cambridge City Council)
  • Subject: Your Cambridge City Council receipt [#1889-6565]
  • Amount Paid: £3.00 per 45-min slot (covered by Erdem on Saturdays)
  • Booking Summary: Court booking at Christ's Pieces for 29 August 2026 09:15 - 10:00
  • Support Email: sportpayments@cambridge.gov.uk
  • Support Telephone: +44 1223 458637

💡 Why both steps are essential:

1. Proof of Booking: Casual players sometimes linger on empty courts. Having your receipt open in your email lets you politely show you have reserved that court and time.

2. Gate Code Access: Electronic Codelocks keypads require the active PIN. If the person who booked is parking or walking from Petersfield Mansions, having the code shared in WhatsApp ensures nobody is stuck waiting outside the fence.

📋 Step-by-Step After Booking

1. Verify Email Receipt

Check your email right after checkout. Confirm the date (e.g. Saturday), court number, and 45-minute time window.

2. Grab PIN & Share to WhatsApp

Open the Play Tennis app / confirmation. Copy the gate PIN and post it in the private WhatsApp group with the court number and time.

3. Unlock & Enjoy

Arrive on time, enter * followed by the PIN on the gate keypad, and start playing!
